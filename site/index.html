What the twelve minutes contains

Same six segments every week, generated only from positions you actually hold. Nothing about a stock you don't own.

01

Where you stand

Total value, week's change, and which accounts drove it — stated, not charted.

02

Your movers

The two or three positions that actually changed your number, and why they moved.

03

Filings and earnings

Results from companies you hold, translated into what it means for your stake.

04

Risk drift

Concentration, sector overlap and cash levels sliding away from where you set them.

05

Process you need to know

Vesting dates, dividend and ex-div dates, tax-loss windows, rebalancing triggers.

06

Before Friday

The short list of things worth a decision this week — and what happens if you skip them.

Read-only, and we mean it

Accounts link through the same read-only aggregation layer your budgeting app uses. slinon can see positions and transactions. It cannot move money, place an order, or change a setting.

  • Read-only by architecture — no trading or transfer permissions exist in our integration
  • Credentials never touch our servers; tokens are bank-grade encrypted at rest
  • Your holdings are never sold, shared, or used to train third-party models
  • Delete your data and every generated episode in one click, any time
+Is this financial advice?

No. Episodes explain what happened in your portfolio and what's scheduled. They don't tell you to buy or sell, and we're not licensed to.

+Where does the analysis come from?

Market data, filings and company disclosures, matched against your positions, then written and narrated. Sources are listed in the episode notes.

+What if my accounts don't connect?

You can add holdings manually — ticker and size is enough — and mix manual with linked accounts in one episode.

+Can I change the length or focus?

Yes. Pick five, twelve or twenty-five minutes, and mute topics you don't care about. The structure stays the same.

+Which app does it play in?

A private feed you paste into Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Overcast or anything else. No new app required.
